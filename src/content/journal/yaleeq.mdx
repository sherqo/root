Finding an internship was hard and it sucked, so [**Youssef Tarek**](https://www.yousseftarek.tech/) and I started [**Yaleeq**](https://www.yaleeq.net) as a place to list internships. It grew into automatically finding them from across the internet, with companies posting directly through their own accounts. We expanded further with profiles for every user, analyzing their CVs to match them with the right opportunity. We hit **700+ users**.

We stopped because it felt like a **waste of time**, honestly. No real internships were being listed. Almost everything was just recommendations, and even the listed ones were mostly for show or data collection.

<Image src={yaleeqCard} alt="Yaleeq card reading Your Career Starts Here" />

Still, it was an _amazing_ experience. We learned how to work as a team, organize work, set priorities, and actually move a real product to market: marketing on [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/company/yaleeq) to 600+ followers, pitching the idea to anyone who'd listen, pitching to investors, and working all night on the product. **Life-changing**, even though we stopped.
